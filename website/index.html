FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Still have questions?

Answers to the questions we hear most about how we work and what it costs.

What kind of tasks can I send you?

Business and admin work, legal and IP reviews, CAC business registration, operations and logistics, creator support and tax readiness. Specialist add-ons such as social media management and bookkeeping are priced separately. If you are not sure whether something fits, send it and a coordinator will scope it with you before any work starts.

Who does the work?

A member of our team with the right skills does the work, and a second person checks it before it is delivered to you.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. You can move up or down between plans, and the change applies from your next billing month.

Is there a setup fee?

No. You pay the monthly price of your plan and nothing extra to get started.

What happens if I use more tasks than my plan allows?

We let you know before you reach your limit. You can upgrade your plan, or we send a quote for the extra work and only start once you approve it.

Do you offer refunds?

If something is not right with a delivered task, tell us and we will correct it. For refund requests, contact our team and we will review it with you.

Can I cancel my plan anytime?

Yes. Cancel whenever you like and your plan stays active until the end of the month you have paid for.
