1. What cookies are and how we use them

Cookies are small text files that a website saves on your device. We use as few as we can. B&A Essential services ("B&A Essentials") uses cookies on bandaessentials.com and the B&A Essentials platform to keep you signed in, protect the site and remember simple choices.

We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not sell data collected through cookies. We do not use analytics cookies at launch. If we add them, we will ask for your consent first and update this notice.

Cookies that are strictly necessary for the site to work or to keep it secure do not need your consent. For every other cookie, we ask you first.

Payment pages. When you pay, you go to a page run by our payment provider, Paystack or Flutterwave. They set their own cookies, which we do not control. Please read their cookie notices.

Messaging. Messages we send you through providers such as Termii do not use cookies on our site.

3. Your choices and contact

Choosing. The first time you visit, a banner asks you to accept or reject non-essential cookies. The choices are equal, and nothing non-essential is switched on before you choose. You can change your mind at any time through "Cookie settings" in the footer of every page.

Your browser. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block strictly necessary cookies, you will not be able to sign in or use the platform.

Changes. We may update this notice. We will post the new version here with its date, and ask for your choices again if we add a new kind of non-essential cookie.

Contact. Questions about cookies: dpo@bandaessentials.com. Support: support@bandaessentials.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
