1. Who we are and what this policy covers

This policy explains how [registered name of B&A Essentials entity] ("B&A Essentials", "we", "us") collects and uses personal data when you visit bandaessentials.com or use the B&A Essential Services platform and services. We are a company registered in Nigeria BN No. 7410773, with our registered office at Plot 136 Alake Onile-Ere Crescent, Gbagada, Lagos, Nigeria. We follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the guidance of the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

Our role. For your account, billing, security and website data, we decide why and how the data is used, so we are the "controller". For documents and information you send us to work on, which may include personal data about your staff, customers or others, you decide what to send and what we do with it, and we handle it on your instructions as your "processor".

Who to contact. Our data protection contact is reachable at dpo@bandaessentials.com. General support is at support@bandaessentials.com. Our postal address is Plot 136 Alake Onile-Ere Crescent, Gbagada, Lagos, Nigeria.

A shorter notice is shown at sign-up and upload. If anything in it differs from this policy, tell us.

6. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you have rights over your personal data. You can ask us to:

  • tell you whether we hold your data and give you a copy;
  • correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your data, where we no longer need it or have no lawful reason to keep it;
  • restrict how we use your data while a question about it is settled;
  • stop using your data where we rely on our legitimate interests, and stop marketing at any time;
  • give you your data in a common format, or send it to another provider, where the law allows;
  • withdraw any consent you gave, which does not affect what we did before; and
  • review any decision made about you by automated means.

How to use them. Email dpo@bandaessentials.com or use the request option in your account. We may need to check it is you, and we will ask only for what is needed. We reply within thirty (30) days, and tell you if we need more time or cannot do what you ask and why. Requests are free unless they are clearly unfounded or excessive.

Data in your documents. If your request concerns data about other people in documents you gave us, we may refer you to the business that sent the document, or act on that business's instructions.

Complaints. If you are unhappy with how we handled your data, please tell us first at dpo@bandaessentials.com. You also have the right to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng).

7. Security, children, marketing and changes

Security. We protect your data with sign-in verification, multi-factor authentication for all staff, role-based access so people see only what their task needs, private encrypted storage for documents, file scanning, audit logs and regular backups with tested recovery. No system is completely secure, so please keep your sign-in channel safe.

If something goes wrong. If there is a personal data breach that puts your rights at risk, we will tell the NDPC and the people affected within the time the law requires, and tell you what happened and what to do.

Children. Our services are for businesses and adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data about children. If you think we have, please tell us at dpo@bandaessentials.com.

Marketing. We send marketing messages only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time using the link in a message or by emailing support@bandaessentials.com. Service messages, such as reminders and billing notices, are not marketing and you cannot opt out of those while you use the service.

Cookies. See our Cookie Notice for how we use cookies and how to choose.

Changes. We may update this policy. We will post the new version here with its date, and tell you directly if the change is significant.

Contact. Data protection: dpo@bandaessentials.com. Support: support@bandaessentials.com. Website: bandaessentials.com.
